# 🚗 SAP MM - Auto Spare Parts P2P Implementation

**Company:** AutoTech Pvt. Ltd | Nagpur
**Domain:** Manufacturing & Procurement | SAP S/4HANA

### 🎯 Business Problem
The company faced delays in procurement due to manual purchasing, inaccurate stock maintenance, and no proper vendor tracking system.

### ✅ My End-to-End Solution
Implemented complete Procure-to-Pay (P2P) cycle in SAP S/4HANA:

**1. Master Data Creation**
- Material Master (MM01): Created 50+ materials - Brake Pad, Engine Oil, Air Filter, Clutch Plate, Tyre
- Vendor Master (XK01): Bosch, Castrol, MRF
- Info Record & Source List

**2. Procurement Cycle (P2P)**
- Purchase Requisition (ME51N)
- Request for Quotation (ME41)
- Quotation Maintenance (ME47) & Comparison
- Purchase Order (ME21N) with GST Tax Calculation
- Goods Receipt (MIGO) with Quality Check
- Invoice Verification (MIRO)

**3. Inventory & Reporting**
- Stock Overview (MB52, MMBE)
- PO Tracking (ME2N, ME2M)

### 📈 Business Impact
- Reduced procurement cycle time by 70%
- Achieved 99% stock accuracy
- Automated GST calculation

**T-Codes Used:** MM01, XK01, ME51N, ME41, ME47, ME21N, MIGO, MIRO, MB52, ME2N
